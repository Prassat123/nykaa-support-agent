If a payment fails but money is debited, the amount is automatically refunded to the source account within 5 to 7 business days. The customer can retry payment from the order page within 30 minutes before the order is auto-cancelled. Customers should not retry more than 3 times on the same card in one hour to avoid a temporary bank block. If the refund does not arrive in 7 business days, the customer should contact support with the transaction ID.
